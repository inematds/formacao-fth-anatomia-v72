Lesson 3 · 15 min

Degrees of freedom, joints, and limits

"23 to 43 degrees of freedom" is marketing; the joint tree in the file is the body your controller will command. In this lesson you list the G1's 29 joints with limits in degrees and maximum torque, understand why the leg has six, and check that the number matches the actuators.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The body tree: pelvis, legs, waist, and arms
  2. 2Why the leg has 6 joints and in what order
  3. 3Limits are in radians in the file, in degrees in your head
  4. 4Degrees of freedom in the file × on the manufacturer's spec sheet

Goal: list the G1's joints with limits in degrees and maximum torque, and check that the number of articulated joints matches nu.

01 · The 29 joints of the G1 from Menagerie

Each block is a branch of the tree that starts at the pelvis. Click to see the joints.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

12 in the legs + 3 in the waist + 14 in the arms = 29 = nu.

02 · How to read a line of the tree

Click each step.

1. Name

left_hip_roll_joint: left leg, hip, roll rotation (tilting sideways). Pitch bends forward and backward; yaw turns around the vertical axis.

The indentation in the output shows what hangs from what: the ankle depends on the knee, which depends on the hip.

2. Limit

The file stores range="-0.087267 2.8798" in radians; the script converts it: -5° to 165° at the knee.

The hip roll is asymmetric and mirrored: left from -30° to 170°, right from -170° to 30°. The leg opens far outward and only a little inward, where the other leg is.

3. Maximum torque

The joint's actuatorfrcrange attribute caps what the motor can do: 139 N·m at the knee, 88 at the hip pitch, 50 at the ankle, 25 at the shoulder, 5 at the wrist.

This is the model's limit. Resellers quote 90 N·m (base) and 120 N·m (EDU) for the real G1's knee (reported): one more point where the file and the robot can differ.

4. Check

The total number of joints (njnt=30) includes the free base. The number that matters to the controller is the number of articulated joints, which must match the number of actuators.

If they don't match, there is a passive joint (with no motor) or a motor outside a joint: both change your controller.

Last line of the scriptRESUMO: 29 juntas articuladas + 1 base flutuante · nu=29 · bate com nu: sim

abrir.py from lesson 2 prints juntas: 30 (1 base flutuante + 29 articuladas). If you use njnt=30 as the number of motors, you build a command vector with 30 slots and MuJoCo rejects it: ctrl has nu=29. Count only the articulated joints.
